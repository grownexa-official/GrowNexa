🛡️ Official Contact Notice

For payments, projects, website work or important service requests, always contact us through the official details shown on this page. Never trust random accounts claiming to represent GrowthService.

⚠️ Stay Safe Online

Important safety rules before contacting anyone online

🚫

Never Share Your Password

Never send Gmail, Instagram, Facebook, gaming or any other account password to anyone.

🔐

Never Share OTP

No genuine support person should ask you to send your OTP, verification code or recovery code.

💳

Be Careful With Payments

Verify the recipient and service details before making any payment. Never rush because of pressure or threats.

🎣

Avoid Phishing Links

Do not open suspicious links, APK files or unknown login pages sent by strangers.

☠️

Beware Of Scams

Fake support accounts, fake offers, fake giveaways and fake investment claims are common online.

👤

Verify Identity

Before sharing personal or financial information, make sure you are communicating through an official contact channel.

🧑‍💻

Cyber Crime Awareness

If someone threatens, cheats, impersonates or steals information from you, preserve evidence and report it through appropriate official cyber-crime channels.

📸

Keep Proof

Keep screenshots, transaction records, usernames, email addresses and relevant messages if you suspect fraud.

📱

Do Not Give Remote Access

Never give unknown people remote access to your phone or computer just because they claim to be support staff.

⚠️

No Guaranteed Results

Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed followers, views, income, account recovery or instant success.
